Returns and refunds

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order until it is shipped. The books go back to stock and any payment made online is refunded.

Returning a delivered order

Request a return within 7 days of delivery if your book arrived damaged or defective, if we sent the wrong item, if it is not as described, or if you ordered by mistake. One return request can be made per order.

  1. Open the order in My orders and choose Request a return.
  2. Pick a reason and add a few details.
  3. We review the request and update its status in your order page: Approved, Rejected or Refunded.

Refunds

Approved returns are refunded to the original payment method once the books are received back and checked.
