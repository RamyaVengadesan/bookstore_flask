Privacy policy

What we collect

Your name, email address, phone number and delivery addresses when you create an account or place an order, together with your order history, wishlist and reviews. We do not collect card or UPI details in this demo.

How we use it

To process and deliver orders, send order updates, answer your messages and keep your account secure. If you subscribe to the newsletter we use your email to send new arrivals and offers, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

How we protect it

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, never in plain text. Account pages and order details are only visible to you and to store staff who need them to fulfil your order.

Your choices

You can update your details from My account, or contact us to ask for your data to be corrected or removed.
